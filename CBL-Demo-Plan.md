# Live Demo Plan — CBL Presentation (July 7, 2026)

## When to Demo

**After slide 6 (The Interpreter Role).** You've explained the problem (slides 2–3), what TrueRate is (slides 3–4), and how the data works (slides 5–6). Now show it live before moving to "What This Means for CBL" — the committee sees the product first, then hears what it does for them. Everything after the demo lands harder.

Say: *"Let me show you what this looks like in practice."*

---

## The Flow (3–4 Minutes)

### 1. Homepage — 30 seconds

`truerateliberia.com`

- Point at the **indicators strip** at the top — "These are live CBL figures, updated automatically"
- Gesture at the news feed layout — "This is what Yahoo Finance does for America. This is what we do for Liberia."
- Don't click anything yet. Let the layout speak.

### 2. Analytics Terminal — 60 seconds (the showstopper)

`truerateliberia.com/analytics`

- Show the **ticker tape** scrolling across the top — "Every one of these is a CBL statistical series"
- Scroll down to a section — exchange rates or monetary policy
- Click into a **trend chart** — "Interactive. Any Liberian with a phone can see this. Right now, this data lives in a PDF table."
- Point at the **"Source: CBL"** attribution — "Your name is on every figure"

### 3. Markets — 30 seconds

`truerateliberia.com/markets`

- Show the **live FX table** (USD/LRD, EUR/LRD, GHS/LRD) — "These update in real time"
- Scroll to the **CBL macro charts** (interest rates, money supply, debt breakdown) — "This is your data, visualized"

### 4. A News Article — 30 seconds

`truerateliberia.com/news` → click any article

- Point at the **data box** with CBL figures and source attribution
- Say: *"Every number in this article was verified against CBL's published data before publication. Automatically."*

### 5. Data Integrity Reports — 30 seconds (the credibility punch)

`truerateliberia.com/reports`

- Show the two reports — "We already found a data-entry error in the MPR series and 24 anomalies across other CBL databanks"
- Say: *"Our system doesn't just publish your data — it catches errors before the public sees them. This protects CBL."*
- This proves TrueRate adds value beyond republishing

### 6. Mobile View — 15 seconds

- Resize the browser window to phone width, or pull out your phone
- Say: *"Most Liberians access the internet on their phones. This was built for that."*

## Then Resume the Deck

Slide 7: **"What This Means for CBL"** — now they've seen it. Dissemination, literacy, stability, misinformation — every card on that slide has a visual they just watched.

---

## Rules

1. **Never apologize** for anything on the site. If something isn't perfect, skip it.
2. **Don't over-explain.** Let the visuals do the work. One sentence per stop.
3. **Stay on the pages listed.** Don't explore. If they ask to see something else, say "I'd love to walk you through that after."
4. **If WiFi is slow**, have screenshots on your phone as backup. But a live demo is 10x more convincing than screenshots.
5. **The Reports page is your closer.** If they're skeptical, that page proves you're not just republishing — you're quality-controlling their data for free.
