Brand Guidelines · Social Media · v2

The TrueRate
Social System

How TrueRate — Liberia's financial intelligence platform — shows up on Instagram, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, and TikTok. One system, ten formats, zero guesswork.

01

Wordmark

On dark / photography — lime
On surface / light — black
Placement
Photo-led cards (Breaking, Article, Quote, Story, Video Cover): bottom-right, on the baseline strip shared with the photo credit. Data cards (Big Stat, Markets, Daily Rate, Event, Explainer): upper-left header row, opposite the date/section meta.
Size
64px tall on 1080-wide canvases. Never smaller than 48px, never larger than 72px.
Clear space
Keep at least the height of the mark clear on all sides. Nothing else shares the mark's corner.
Asset
Always use the trimmed wordmark file — the untrimmed logo carries transparent padding that breaks sizing.

Color

Dark
#050D11
Primary dark background — site `brand-dark` token.
Lime
#BFEA36
Brand accent. Chips, wordmark, key figures. Use sparingly — it should feel like a signal, not wallpaper.
Surface
#F8F9FA
Light variant background (Broadsheet) — site `brand-surface` token.
Negative
#E11B22
Negative change only — site `neg` token. Never decorative.
Positive
#00A757
Positive change — site `pos` token. (On dark, lime may play this role.)
Card
#040F18
Panels and cards on dark — site `brand-card` token.
Charcoal
#1E1E1E
Approved neutral — site footer background. Secondary bands and utility surfaces only; never a primary card canvas.

04

Layout System

Canvas
1080×1350 (4:5) feed · 1080×1920 (9:16) story · 1920×1080 (16:9) video cover
Margins
48px on all content sides. Headlines, chips, credits share the same left edge.
Baseline strip
Photo-led cards: credit (left) + wordmark (right) on one row, 28px from the bottom edge. Data cards: source line only (wordmark lives in the header). Content block sits clear above it.
Photography
Full-bleed, color, editorial. Vertical gradient scrim for legibility — never a solid box behind text.
Story safe zones
Keep content 250px clear of top and bottom on 9:16 (platform UI overlays).
Variants
Terminal (dark #050D11, photo-forward, default) · Broadsheet (surface #F8F9FA, editorial). Pick one per post; don't mix within a carousel.

06

Voice & Standards

▲ Do
  • Lead with the number: "Gold Hit US$175 Million in March"
  • Sentence case headlines; specific figures over adjectives
  • Credit every photo; source every figure (CBL · LISGIS · TrueRate Research)
  • Sign off with truerateliberia.com / @truerateliberia
  • Authoritative, plain-language — explain what it means for the reader
▼ Don't
  • No ALL-CAPS headlines, no exclamation marks, no emoji
  • No lime backgrounds — lime is an accent, never a canvas
  • No off-token colors — only site tokens: #050D11 · #040F18 · #BFEA36 · #F8F9FA · #E11B22 · #00A757 · #1E1E1E (neutral)
  • No unsourced numbers, no "experts say"
  • No black-and-white photo treatment (color is the house style)
  • Never stretch, recolor, or restage the wordmark
